01 · FIRST_LAUNCH

It figures out your machine in 800ms.

No model picker. No quantization drop-down. localSpace probes your CPU, GPU, and unified memory, then matches against a registry of 143 GGUF checkpoints — recommending only what will actually run on your hardware.

probe
Detects instruction sets, VRAM, unified memory, and Metal/CUDA/Vulkan backends — without ever touching a server.
match
Recommends models pre-filtered to your VRAM headroom. You see real tok/s estimates before you download a single weight.
load
First prompt streams in under 1.2s from a warm cache. No 14GB downloads to "get started".

04 · WHY_LOCAL

The best thinking tool shouldn't ship your half-formed ideas to a server in Virginia.

localSpace runs everything — the model, the canvas, the WASM extensions — inside a single process on your machine. We don't have servers because we don't want them. We can't see your prompts because the binary you installed doesn't know how to phone home. The license is MIT, the runtime is Rust, and the only network call is the one you make to download a model.

— the localSpace team · sketched in a basement in Berlin, 2024
