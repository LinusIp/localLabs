Who it's for

AI that stays inside your walls.

Nine kinds of work localSpace is built for. In each one, documents, messages and answers move freely inside, and nothing crosses the wall.

Without a wall, what you type goes off to someone else's computer. localSpace keeps it inside yours.

Works today in the app as it is now Coming soon being built

The bank

Banks, payment companies and other regulated organisations

Our staff want AI help. Our customers' data can't leave the country.

  • Summarise a regulation or contract pasted into the chat Works today
  • Draft replies to customers and check a notice's wording Works today
  • Every employee on the bank's own server, with IT choosing the models Coming soon

Why local: customer and transaction data stays on the bank's own machines.

The clinic

Clinics and medical offices

Patient letters all day, and the patients' details never leave this building.

  • Referral notes, patient letters and appointment reminders, from the doctor's own notes Works today
  • Answers in Russian and Uzbek, for translations Coming soon
  • One office computer serving the whole team Coming soon

Why local: patient information stays in the building.

The law office

Lawyers, notaries and in-house legal teams

Client files are confidential. They don't go into anyone's chatbot.

  • Read a clause and say in plain words who it favours Works today
  • Draft letters to clients and to the other side Works today
  • Open a contract's PDF or Word file directly Coming soon

Why local: confidentiality is the job itself, and the text never leaves the office's computer.

Lessons to plan, pupils' work to read, and a connection we can't count on.

  • Quiz questions from a text, at the pupils' level Works today
